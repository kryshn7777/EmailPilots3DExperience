# Yard props: credits

`props.glb` and the `atlas-*.webp` maps are built by `blender/make_yard_props.py`. Some of the
props are Poly Haven models, released under **CC0 1.0** (https://polyhaven.com/license). Each one was
downloaded at 1k, decimated, and re-baked into the shared atlas. CC0 does not require attribution,
