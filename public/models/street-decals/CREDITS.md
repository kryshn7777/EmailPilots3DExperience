# Street decals: credits

`decals-color.webp` (2048 px, RGBA) and `decals-nrm.webp` (1024 px: normal x/y, roughness) are drawn
entirely from code by `scripts/make-street-decals.py` (PIL + numpy): shapes, noise, wear, cracks and the
height field the normals come from. No photographs or third-party artwork are used.

The road lettering (BUS LANE, SLOW, GAS, ELEC, G) and the manhole cover's ring text are rasterised from
DejaVu Sans Bold (Bitstream Vera licence / public-domain changes; free to use, embed and redistribute in
rendered form). The rasterised glyphs are part of the images; no font file ships with the site.

Produced for this project; released with it.
