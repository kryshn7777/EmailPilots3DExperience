# Cars: sources and licences

Every file in this folder is generated by `blender/make_cars.py` (geometry in `blender/bld/cars.py`,
tiers and baked maps in `blender/bld/car_bake.py`). No third-party model, texture or scan is used:
the occlusion, grime, curvature and normal maps are baked in Cycles from the project's own
high-poly build, and the grime noise is Blender's procedural noise.

## Survey of downloadable vehicles (2026-11-04)

Looked for realistic road cars licensed for commercial web use (CC0, CC-BY, MIT, Apache). None
was both realistic and usable, so the procedural cars were upgraded instead.

| candidate | licence evidence | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poly Haven `covered_car` | CC0 (api.polyhaven.com/info/covered_car; 12.6k tris) | a car under a dust sheet: a garage prop, not traffic. Poly Haven has no other vehicle in its 521 models (searched names, tags, categories) |
| Khronos glTF-Sample-Assets `ToyCar` | CC0-1.0 (Models/ToyCar/metadata.json) | a small toy car in a showcase scene, not a road vehicle |
| Khronos `CesiumMilkTruck` | sample asset | low-poly stylised truck |
| Khronos `CarConcept` | not used (licence excludes this use per the brief) | rejected |
| three.js example `ferrari.glb` | not licensed for reuse | rejected |
| Kenney Car Kit, Quaternius vehicle packs, OpenGameArt | CC0 | stylised low-poly; not the realistic look wanted |
| Sketchfab, BlenderKit | need accounts and tokens | skipped |
