# Birds: sources and licences

Everything in this folder is generated by `blender/make_birds.py` from the descriptions in
`blender/bld/birds.py` (see `blender/README.md`, "Birds"): the rock pigeons, the starlings and
the black-headed gull are modelled procedurally in headless Blender (lofted bodies, every feather
built from its vane outline), coloured with procedural patterns written for this project and
baked with Cycles.

| what | source | licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| geometry, feather layout, colour patterns, bakes | `blender/bld/birds.py`, `blender/make_birds.py` (this project) | the project's own |

No external model, texture or scan is used. Poly Haven (CC0) was checked for a usable bird model
(api.polyhaven.com, models) and has none; Sketchfab and BlenderKit material is not used.
Plumage, proportions and flight (wing-beat rates, wing bars, the pigeon's raised-V glide) follow
general field-guide knowledge of *Columba livia*, *Sturnus vulgaris* and *Chroicocephalus
ridibundus*; no reference image is copied.
