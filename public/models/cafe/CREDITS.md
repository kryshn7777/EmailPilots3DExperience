# Café terrace props: sources and licences

Everything in this folder (`cafe.glb`, the `atlas-*.webp` maps and `manifest.json`) is generated by
`blender/make_cafe_props.py`: the bentwood chairs, the two laid marble tables, the three market
parasols, the planters, the chalk menu board and the festoon post are modelled procedurally in
headless Blender, coloured with procedural patterns written for this project (cane weave, marble
veins, wood grain, fabric weave, the chalk lettering as text meshes) and baked with Cycles.

| what | source | licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| geometry, materials, bakes | `blender/make_cafe_props.py` (this project), with the helpers of `blender/make_yard_props.py` | the project's own |

No external model, texture or scan is used. The sign over the door, the awning stripes and the
delivery's envelope are canvas textures drawn at runtime (`src/webgl/actors/CafeTerrace.ts`); the
lettering uses the system's serif font.
